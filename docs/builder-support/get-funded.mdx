## Uniswap Grants & Builder Support Programs

Whether you're prototyping a new idea or scaling your next venture, the Uniswap ecosystem provides grants and support programs for developers at every stage. 

Following the passage of the UNIfication governance proposal in December 2025, Uniswap's funding structure has evolved. While the Uniswap Foundation continues to deploy grants from its remaining budget, future ecosystem funding will come from Uniswap Labs through an annual growth budget of 20 million UNI starting in 2026. Most Foundation operational teams have transitioned to Labs to unify protocol development and ecosystem growth under a single strategy.

## Before You Apply

1. **Deploy to Unichain (and/or v4).** Some programs will consider early prototypes, but being deployed on Unichain and/or Uniswap v4 will give you an advantage. 
    
    Verify that your contract is deployed and functional on Unichain.

2. **Provide documentation.** Make sure that your application includes clear documentation including setup instructions, project goals, and demo materials.

    Ensure that your application includes a README.md and a working demo.

3. **Highlight your impact.** Measure and include metrics like user adoption, transaction volume, or community engagement.

    Share early data and qualitative user feedback to strengthen your application!

## Uniswap Hook Incubator

The Uniswap Hook Incubator is an intensive, cohort-based program designed to help builders master v4 hook development. Since its launch in 2024, the program has onboarded nearly 500 developers from 50+ countries, resulting in 420+ hooks shipped across seven cohorts.

The 9-week program (8 weeks of curriculum + 1-week Hookathon) provides hands-on training, expert mentorship, and culminates in a capstone competition with prizes. Participants build innovative hooks ranging from MEV protection systems to advanced liquidity management tools.

[Apply for the Uniswap Hook Incubator](https://atrium.academy/uniswap)

**Ideal candidates:**

- Are experienced Solidity developers
- Have DeFi experience
- Are passionate about exploring and building new primitives

**What you get:**

- Fully covered tuition (funded by Uniswap Foundation)
- Up to $40k in prizes for top capstone projects
- Access to DeFi experts and the largest Uniswap v4 builder community
- Technical mentorship from v4 core contributors
- Weekly office hours and guest lectures

**Program highlights:**
- 55% retention rate (far above the 20% industry average for similar programs)
- 420+ hooks shipped by graduates
- Alumni projects have secured venture funding, including Semantic Layer and Tenor
- Four additional cohorts planned for 2026 with specialized themes

## Hook Design Lab

The Uniswap v4 Hook Design Lab is a pilot program structured to support emerging DeFi builders, helping teams bring new ecosystem primitives from idea to mainnet with technical mentorship, GTM support, and milestone-based funding. 

[Apply to the Hook Design Lab.](https://share.hsforms.com/1y57UxXGGSrKc7d8v5opZzwsdca9)

**Ideal projects:**

- Bring a unique approach to capital efficiency, LP experience, target asset classes, and/or composability
- Create public goods and infrastructure for the community to build on
- Demonstrate the potential for ecosystem-wide impact

A full list of builder requests can be found [here](https://www.uniswapfoundation.org/blog/uniswap-v4-supercharging-defi-across-chains).

**What you get:**

- Grant funding for GTM and growth campaigns
- Technical, product design, and marketing support
- Holistic integration and routing support

## UFSF Audit Subsidies

Ready to launch? The Uniswap Foundation Security Fund provides access to best-in-class security audits at reduced or no cost, streamlining the process through a vetted pool of providers with transparent pricing and quality standards. The program is managed by Areta.

[Apply for UFSF audit subsidies](https://areta.fillout.com/UFSF)

**What you get:**

- Access to premium audit slots with top providers
- Standardized deliverables and quality assurance
- For subsidy recipients: Up to 100% of audit costs covered

## 2026 Grant Outlook

**Important context:** Following the December 2025 passage of the UNIfication proposal, Uniswap's grant structure is transitioning. The Uniswap Foundation will continue to deploy its remaining budget ($99.8 million allocated for 2025-2026) consistent with its mission. After this budget is deployed, future ecosystem grants will come from Uniswap Labs through an annual growth budget of 20 million UNI distributed quarterly beginning January 1, 2026.

This unified approach consolidates ecosystem support, governance, and developer relations under Uniswap Labs while maintaining the Foundation's focus on grants and incentives through a small dedicated team.

For the latest information on grant programs and funding opportunities, visit [uniswapfoundation.org/build](https://www.uniswapfoundation.org/build).
